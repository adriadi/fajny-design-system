# B-017 · Build Input

## Owner's answers to B-016

1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A, 6A, 7A, 8A, 9A, 10A: every recommendation. They're written up as decisions 59 and 60 in `DECISIONS.md` on `main` (`git pull`). Checkbox went to the parking lot.

In short:
- **Style:** outlined, label above.
- **Shape:** white `color-surface` fill, `radius-md` 8px, at least 48px high.
- **New role:** an eleventh text role `field`, Inter 400, 16px at every width.
- **Disabled:** native `disabled` plus `opacity.disabled`.
- **Show/hide:** the password button is part of Input.
- **Marking:** "(optional)" on optional fields only.
- **Scope:** light surfaces only. No checkbox, no read-only.

Everything else follows your B-016 proposal as written: parts and linking, the states table, sizes, the error pattern, and the class names `fds-field`, `fds-field__label`, `fds-input`, `fds-field__help`, `fds-field__error`, `fds-field__action`.

   - Only tokens, no literal values: the "components use only tokens" check must pass.
